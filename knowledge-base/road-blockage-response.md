# Road Blockage Response — Sample Reference Document

> Sample reference material prepared for the HillSense AI demonstration. Not an official government publication.

Hill roads are frequently blocked by landslides, rockfall, fallen trees, and washouts, particularly on national highways and state highways during the monsoon.

## First response for travellers at a blockage

- Stop at a safe, visible location well before the debris field
- Switch on hazard lights; help place warning markers at both approaches
- Check whether anyone is trapped under debris — call 112 immediately
- Do not attempt to clear large boulders or trees without machinery

## While waiting for clearance

- Park on stable ground away from cut slopes and overhangs
- Keep the road edge clear so machinery and ambulances can pass
- Conserve fuel, water, and phone battery
- Follow instructions from police, PWD, or BRO crews on site

## Coordinating clearance

- Report the exact landmark or chainage to the local administration
- Request suitable machinery: excavator for debris, cranes for vehicles
- Divert traffic via the notified alternate route where one exists
- Reopen only after the slope above the road has been inspected

## Special cautions

- A freshly cleared stretch can fail again — watch for new falls
- Night clearing is riskier; use proper lighting and spotters
- Washed-out road surfaces may look intact — test edges on foot first
