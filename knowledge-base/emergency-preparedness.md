# Emergency Preparedness — Sample Reference Document

> Sample reference material prepared for the HillSense AI demonstration. Not an official government publication.

Mountain communities face isolated geographies where external help takes time to arrive. Household and community preparedness materially reduces losses.

## Household emergency kit

- Drinking water (3 days) and dry food
- Torch, candles, matches, power bank
- First-aid kit with personal medicines
- Whistle to signal for help
- Radio (battery/hand-crank) for alerts
- Copies of important documents in a waterproof pouch

## Family plan

- Agree on two meeting points: one near home, one outside the locality
- Save local emergency numbers: 112 (all emergencies), 108 (ambulance), 1077 (helpline)
- Keep vehicle fuel above half during monsoon season
- Know the nearest safe high ground and the route to reach it on foot

## Community measures

- Village disaster committees should map vulnerable houses and routes
- Practise one evacuation drill before every monsoon
- Identify strong buildings for use as shelters
- Keep clearing drains and culverts before the rains

## During any emergency

- Stay informed; avoid spreading unverified messages
- Keep phone calls short so networks stay available for rescue teams
- Check on elderly neighbours and people living alone
