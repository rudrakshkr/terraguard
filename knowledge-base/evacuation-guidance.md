# Evacuation Guidance — Sample Reference Document

> Sample reference material prepared for the HillSense AI demonstration. Not an official government publication.

Evacuation in hilly terrain is constrained by road capacity, bridge availability, and landslide risk. Decide early, move calmly, and move vertically or along designated safe routes.

## When to evacuate

- Official advisory or order from the district administration
- Visible imminent threat: rising water, active slope movement, fire line advancing
- You are below a dam, slide zone, or steep burnt slope during heavy rain
- When local elders or the village disaster committee advise moving

## Before you leave

- Carry documents, medicines, water, dry food, torch, and phone power bank
- Shut off gas and electricity at the mains if safe to do so
- Lock the house; inform neighbours of your destination
- Help elderly, children, and persons with disabilities first

## Where to go

- Move to designated shelters or higher stable ground
- Do not shelter in buildings at the base of slopes or on riverbanks
- Follow the route announced by authorities — shortcuts can cross hazard zones
- Keep to the valley-wall side of the road, not the valley floor

## During movement

- Travel in groups; keep a headcount
- Stop at safe distances when crossing exposed slopes or bridges
- Listen for further advisories on radio or local announcements

## After the emergency

- Return only when authorities declare the area safe
- Report missing persons and damage to the local administration
- Check structures for cracks before reoccupying
