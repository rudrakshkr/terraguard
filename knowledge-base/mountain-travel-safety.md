# Mountain Travel Safety — Sample Reference Document

> Sample reference material prepared for the HillSense AI demonstration. Not an official government publication.

Himachal's roads and trails are spectacular but demanding. Most visitor emergencies are preventable with route awareness and conservative decisions.

## Before you travel

- Check the weather forecast and any district travel advisories
- Avoid night driving on hill roads; plan to reach before dark
- Share your route and expected arrival with someone reliable
- Keep fuel topped up — pumps can be far apart

## On the road

- Give hill-side room to oncoming traffic; the vehicle going uphill has right of way
- Use low gear on descents; never ride the brakes for long stretches
- Stop only at designated viewpoints or wide stable shoulders
- During rain, watch for fresh rockfall on cut slopes and muddy patches

## Weather awareness

- In the monsoon, treat every nala crossing and cut slope as active hazard
- In winter, carry chains and check avalanche advisories for high passes
- In dry summer, avoid parking on dry grass; forest fire risk is extreme

## If you encounter a hazard

- Do not stop inside a rockfall or slide zone to take photographs
- Report blockages and incidents to the local administration or via the HillSense app
- Help stranded travellers without blocking the carriageway
- Turn back early — mountain conditions worsen faster than forecasts predict
